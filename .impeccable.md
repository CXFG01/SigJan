## Design Context

### Users

Patients—often older, recently discharged, visually impaired, or cognitively
overloaded—need to reconstruct what they actually take. Caregivers need a shared,
attributable handover. Pharmacists need a fast, source-preserving review and an
accountable resolution path.

### Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and quietly human. SignalRx should feel reassuring through clarity rather
than reassurance claims: serious enough for a pharmacist, gentle enough for an anxious
patient, and transparent enough for an investor to trust the architecture.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, warm-neutral interface inspired by a carefully organised medication folder on
a kitchen table: paper-toned surfaces, ink-blue typography, restrained mineral teal,
and amber reserved for uncertainty. The memorable motif is the “medication story”:
source fragments align into one verified timeline. Avoid futuristic AI imagery,
hospital stock photography, glowing gradients, generic dashboard mosaics, excessive
medical red, and decorative network graphs.

### Design Principles

1. Put the next human action before the system interpretation.
2. Make source, certainty, and ownership visible at the point they matter.
3. Use layout and typography before chrome; cards exist only for records or actions.
4. Keep patient surfaces spacious and forgiving, while professional surfaces are
   compact but never cryptic.
5. Never let colour, motion, or visual sophistication imply clinical certainty.

## Frontend thesis

**Visual thesis:** A tactile, paper-and-ink medication record—warm, composed, and
precise—where fragmented sources visibly resolve into one reviewed story.

**Landing content plan:** Full-canvas promise and medication-story visual; a concise
three-step workflow; a source-and-human-review proof section; an explicit safety
boundary; final role-based call to action.

**Interaction thesis:** A gentle source-to-record entrance sequence; progressive
evidence and field disclosure using grid-row transitions; a persistent journey rail
whose active state advances as confirmation and professional review complete. All
motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
